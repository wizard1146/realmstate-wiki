A house can send aid to another house of its own state: gold, food, materials, soldiers, horses, aether or explorable acres. It is how a state helps a member who is short, or builds one up.

Sending aid

  • Aid leaves your house at once and arrives {{aid_ticks}} ticks later.
  • {{aid_loss_pct}}% is lost on the way.
  • Only to a house of your own state.
  • The order form shows exactly how much will arrive before you send it.

The aid tax

To stop a state pouring everything into one house, a house that has received more aid than it gave pays a tax on what it receives. Every house keeps an aid balance: what it has given minus what it has received, counted in gold-value. Goods are valued at {{aid_value_list}} gold-value each.

  • A house can receive up to {{aid_free_per_acre}} gold-value an acre more than it has given without any tax.
  • Beyond that, each further gold-value an acre adds {{aid_tax_step_pct}}% of tax, up to {{aid_tax_max_pct}}%.
  • Giving builds credit: a house that has given more than it received can later receive that much back tax-free.
  • Balances fade by {{aid_fade_pct}}% every tick, so old aid matters less and less.

Tools can ask for a quote with GET /aid/quote and send with the send_aid command (see API for Tools).
