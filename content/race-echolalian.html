Lore

Out of the School of the Khaphiiri, a single tribe of the jungles rose to uncontested dominance. The Tribe of Mahwe violently extinguished all its competitors and bred its innate thirst for violence into all descendant lines of its sister Tribes. Henceforth, all Junglers bowed to the wisdom of the Khaphiirian Hegemon. In their arrogance, the Hegemon unleashed a devastating curse on their entire people, causing them to rapidly lose their cultural memory. The Junglers adapted, and simply reflect back the cultures, fighting styles and vicissitudes of those who interact with them, earning them the nickname Echolalians.
