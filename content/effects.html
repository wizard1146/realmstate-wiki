Conditional modifiers

A modifier can hold only under conditions, all of which must hold: when = "war" (only while your state is at war) or when = "peace" (only while it isn't); vs = "troll" (only in battles against a house of that race); attack = "massacre" (only on that kind of attack). Race pages show the conditions next to each modifier.

How modifiers stack

Every modifier on a stat adds together: race, personality, buildings, sciences, Colloquium projects, academics, rites, vigils and war standing. The total is applied once.

  • Floor. A total below −100% counts as −100%. The value falls to zero, never below. Construction can cost no gold at all, but a building's extra gold (the Ancestral Hall's, say) is never reduced.
  • Building caps. Each building caps its own bonus (see Buildings). Building efficiency then multiplies it, so efficiency above 100% can pass the cap.
  • No other caps on the total, except these results: at most 80% of a target's ward counts; rite and operation chances stay between 1% and 95%; trade_bonus never goes below zero; and times never fall below an instant.

Unit slots

Every race fills the same 10 unit slots: soldiers (drafted), offense specialist, defense specialist, elite, thief, offense specialist, upgraded, defense specialist, upgraded, elite, upgraded, mercenary, elite++. A race names four base units; the upgraded slots are those units after an upgrade. Thieves have no upgrade.
