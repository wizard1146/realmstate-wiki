The game has an official HTTP API, so tools never need to scrape pages. It sends and receives JSON.

RequestWhat it does
GET /worldAge, current time, tick count, number of houses
GET /rulesThe age's slots, definitions, fingerprints and numbers
GET /rankingsThe 100 largest houses by land, largest first (ties by house id). Not the age result: a state wins the age on score; see The End of an Age and /ages/{age}
GET /houses/{id}Your own house in full; any other house's public facts (name, address, race, land, renown, might)
GET /states/{realm}/{state}A state's houses (public facts)
GET /marketEvery material: producing realms, last clearing price and volume, open orders by price
GET /realms/{realm}A realm's material, and each state's share, house count and leader
GET /stateYour state: members, leader, vote counts, treasury, war recovery ({"share_bp", "until_tick"} or null) and peace_dividend ({"from_tick", "active"}, or null while at war) (signed in)
GET /colloquiumYour state's Colloquium: projects, tiers, knowledge, decay, contributors (signed in)
GET /hallThe Hall of Deeds: every listing, with the character's profile and record, and auction state
GET /offersOffers you've made and offers on your characters (signed in)
GET /ageThe age: tick, when the next tick is due (next_tick_at, ms) and the tick length (tick_ms), ticks left, scoring weights, live state standings, and the result once it has ended
GET /ages/{age}An ended age's result: winner, state scores, house rankings, heirs kept
GET /heirsYour heirs waiting in your account, across ages (signed in)
POST /heirs/recallBring one into your house this age: {"uid": "1-G4"} (signed in)
GET /state/messagesYour state's chat: the last 200 messages, or those after ?after={id} (signed in)
POST /state/messagesPost to your state's chat: {"text": "..."}, 1-1,000 characters (signed in)
GET /liveA WebSocket of live updates: your house's news, your state's chat, and every tick (signed in, with a house). Each message is JSON with a type: news, chat, tick, or resync (reload what you show)
GET /warsWars under way, ceasefires, tribute, war points and past wars
GET /relationsYour state's hostility meters both ways, ceasefire proposals and land ratios; your state's war (signed in)
GET /ordersYour open orders and your state's treasury orders (signed in)
GET /meYour account and your house this age (signed in). The house includes pending, work under way soonest first ({"kind", "what", "count", "started_at", "done_at"}; kind is explore, build, train, medics or army), and prices, your exact costs after modifiers with the most one order may ask for. A cost is floor(base × (10000 + bp) / 10000).
GET /newsYour house's news, newest first (signed in)
GET /reportsIntel reports from your state, newest first; each has a kind (survey, muster, ledgers, archives, couriers, dossier, and from divinations scrying, omens, roads). Add ?target={id} for one house (signed in)
POST /commandsGive an order (signed in; below)

Leave out material in donate and grant to move gold. treasury orders, grant, set_tax, set_research and the war commands are for the state leader only. A list without reserve and ticks takes offers instead of running an auction. Rates are in basis points: 300 means 3%.

Races and personalities are named by identity. Units are numbered by slot: 0 offense, 1 defense, 2 elite, 3 thief, 4 offense+, 5 defense+, 6 elite+; units lists all seven. medics, general, horses, chariots and kind (an id from Attacks; default assault) are optional in an attack. Give both realm and state to ask for a state, or neither (see placement).

Answers

  • 200: done; the body says what happened (for example {"type": "created", "house": 0, "realm": 4, "state": 2, "seat": 1}).
  • 401: sign in first (see Signing In). Orders use your sign-in cookie.
  • 422: the game refused the order; error says why (for example "that house isn't yours").
  • 429: too many orders. You may send a burst of 30, then 5 a second.

Reads may lag the game by up to a quarter of a second. What each request may show follows What You Can See: a successful spy answers with a full report, and nothing else reveals another house's hidden numbers.
