The game has an official HTTP API, so tools never need to scrape pages. It sends and receives JSON.

RequestWhat it does
GET /worldAge, current time, tick count, number of houses
GET /rulesThe age's slots, definitions, fingerprints and numbers
GET /rankingsThe 100 largest houses by land
GET /houses/{id}Your own house in full; any other house's public facts (name, address, race, land)
GET /states/{realm}/{state}A state's houses (public facts)
GET /marketEvery material: producing realms, last clearing price and volume, open orders by price
GET /realms/{realm}A realm's material, and each state's share, house count and leader
GET /stateYour state: members, leader, vote counts, treasury (signed in)
GET /colloquiumYour state's Colloquium: projects, tiers, knowledge, decay, contributors (signed in)
GET /ordersYour open orders and your state's treasury orders (signed in)
GET /meYour account and your house this age (signed in)
GET /newsYour house's news, newest first (signed in)
GET /reportsSpy reports from your state, newest first; add ?target={id} for one house (signed in)
POST /commandsGive an order (signed in; below)

Leave out material in donate and grant to move gold. treasury orders, grant and set_tax are for the state leader only. Rates are in basis points: 300 means 3%.

Races and personalities are named by identity. Units are numbered by slot: 0 offense, 1 defense, 2 elite, 3 thief, 4 offense+, 5 defense+, 6 elite+; units lists all seven. medics, general, horses and chariots are optional in an attack. Give both realm and state to ask for a state, or neither (see placement).

Answers

  • 200: done; the body says what happened (for example {"type": "created", "house": 0, "realm": 4, "state": 2, "seat": 1}).
  • 401: sign in first (see Signing In). Orders use your sign-in cookie.
  • 422: the game refused the order; error says why (for example "that house isn't yours").
  • 429: too many orders. You may send a burst of 30, then 5 a second.

Reads may lag the game by up to a quarter of a second. What each request may show follows What You Can See: a successful spy answers with a full report, and nothing else reveals another house's hidden numbers.
