Realmstate has no passwords. Sign in with Google, or with a passkey you have added.

Google

Choose Sign in with Google. The game only asks Google for your name and email address. Your first sign-in creates your account.

Passkeys

Once signed in, choose Add a passkey. Your device saves a key and unlocks it with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Next time, Sign in with a passkey needs one tap and no Google. You can add a passkey on each device you play on.

Staying signed in

A sign-in lasts 30 days on that browser, or until you sign out.

One house per account

Each account runs one house per age, and only you can give it orders. Your account carries over from age to age.
