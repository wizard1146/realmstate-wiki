Every race has four unit slots: an offense specialist, a defense specialist, an elite and a thief. Each race's units and their numbers are on its race page.

Training

  • Each unit costs gold (its price, times your training cost modifier) and one peasant.
  • Troops are ready exactly {{train_ticks}} hours later. While training they still eat.
  • Up to 1,000,000 at a time.

Attacking

You choose how many troops of each unit slot to send. The battle resolves at once.

  1. Offense = the offense of every unit sent, times your offense modifier, times battle luck (up to ±{{luck_pct}}%).
  2. Defense = the defense of every unit the target has at home, times their defense modifier, plus 1 point per acre they own.
  3. If offense is higher, you win and take {{land_gain_pct}}% of their land. They lose it at once; you receive it when your army returns.
  4. Win or lose, you lose {{attacker_loss_pct}}% of the troops you sent and the target loses {{defender_loss_pct}}% of their troops at home.
  5. Your army comes home {{attack_return_ticks}} hours later, times your return time modifier.

Troops that are away don't defend your house.

What you learn

You see your own offense, whether you won, the land taken and the losses on both sides, but never the defender's defense. The defender's news tells them who attacked and what they lost. To size up a target first, spy on it.

Thieves, the fourth unit slot, don't fight. They are used for spying.
