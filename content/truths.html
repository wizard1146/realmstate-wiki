8 Truths wait to be uncovered this age, each once per world. A house that uncovers one may use it at once, and so may its state; 16 ticks later every house hears which state uncovered it, and may use it too.

  • Chariots: Chariots may be built (a horse, 1 timber and 100 gold each; +2 offense on attacks). Nobody can build them before.
  • Flanked Ambush: The Flanked Ambush attack. Lesser version (subscribers): Lesser Flanked Ambush.
  • Transmutation Rite: The transmute command: 4 of one material and 100 aether make 1 of another, up to 200 a cast. Lesser rite (subscribers): 8 to 1.
  • Dragon Wall: Dragon Walls against dragons. Lesser version (subscribers): Lesser Dragon Walls.

Dragons

From tick 280, every 36 ticks a dragon raids one house, chosen at random among the houses in the top third by land that are not protected. Its strength is 1.2× the median defense at home of those houses. A house whose defense beats it drives it off and earns 300 renown. Otherwise it burns 3% of the buildings, 4% of the troops at home and 2% of the peasants, less what dragon_damage cuts (Dragon Walls). Every other house hears which realm it struck.

World Wonders

Each wonder can be built once per world, by a house that knows its plan (its Truth). The house starts it; houses of its state fund it with gold and its materials; once funded it takes the ticks below to finish. The first to finish owns it, and the world hears. Any other build of it stops, and 50% of what it was given goes back to the house that started it.

Quests

  • Who may be invited: an academic with 2+ attributes, or a general with 1+ traits, of a house with 600+ acres that is not protected and on no quest.
  • The invitation: the world's chance starts at 0.05% a tick and rises, faster and faster, to certainty 140 ticks after the last invitation. The Truth sought stays unnamed.
  • Episodes: the seeker asks a cost (gold, a material, books, aether). Answer within 24 ticks or the quest lapses; the next ask comes 48 ticks later. The whole quest must end within 240 ticks.
  • Risks: each paid episode, the trail goes cold 8% of the time and the seeker dies 1%. After the last, 25% of quests find nothing.
  • Success: 1,000 renown, and the Truth.

Subscribers may use lesser versions of some breakthroughs before they are uncovered. Command: answer_quest (see API for Tools).
