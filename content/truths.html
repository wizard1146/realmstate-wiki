Dragons

From tick {{dragon_from_tick}}, every {{dragon_every_ticks}} ticks a dragon raids one house, chosen at random among the houses in the top third by land that are not protected. The raid is over within the tick: there is no warning. Its strength is {{dragon_strength_pct}}% of the median defense at home of those houses. A house whose defense, raised by its dragon_defense (Dragon Walls), beats it drives it off and earns {{dragon_renown}} renown. Otherwise it burns {{dragon_buildings_pct}}% of the buildings, {{dragon_troops_pct}}% of the troops at home and {{dragon_peasants_pct}}% of the peasants, less what dragon_damage cuts (Dragon Walls).

Each dragon is of a kind, drawn evenly, and has a name of its own: each kind has its own sound. On top of the losses above, a dragon that isn't driven off burns:
