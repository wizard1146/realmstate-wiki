Your state's reports

Every successful intel operation or divination by anyone in your state is shared with the whole state. Thefts, sabotage and hexes make no report. Tools read them at GET /reports (see API): each has a kind of survey, muster, ledgers, archives, couriers, dossier, scrying, omens or roads.

After a battle

An attacker learns its own offense, whether it won, the land taken and both sides' losses. It never learns the defender's defense.
