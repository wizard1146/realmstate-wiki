Your news

Your house keeps a news feed that only you see:

  • Another house attacked you: who, whether they won, the land you lost and the troops you lost.
  • You caught thieves: how many, and which house sent them.
  • Explored land arrived, troops finished training, your army came home.

Your state's spy reports

Every successful spy operation by anyone in your state is shared with the whole state.

After a battle

An attacker learns its own offense, whether it won, the land taken and both sides' losses. It never learns the defender's defense.
