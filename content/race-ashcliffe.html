Lore

Born of the tragic volcanic eruption of Mount Ishiraki, the Ashcliffe were displaced and now traverse the Realms of the World. Known also as the Wanderers, they are a hardy bunch of survivors, relying on their keen sense of camaraderie and huntsmanship to bolster their presence. Faint memories of the technology they once possessed help them conquer lands faster.
