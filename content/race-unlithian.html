Lore

All Unlithians acknowledge one Truth: everything has a price, and for most peoples, that price is Gold. The politics of an Unlithian State is steeped in the flow of money, and so of trade. Theirs is a world of clinking coins and secret handshakes. Even when they make war, their armies are paid so handsomely that their foes falter, knowing the cost they will pay.
