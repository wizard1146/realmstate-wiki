The Colloquium is your state's shared research. Every house can give books; every house benefits. It has its own page in the game.

How it works

  • Your state's leader chooses one project to research.
  • Any member can give books of any category to it. Books are the only thing that counts.
  • Each project has three tiers. When the state's books reach a tier, its effect applies to every house in the state, including houses that join later.
  • Some projects are open only to states in a realm that makes their material, such as Mining Knowledge for bauxite realms.
  • For every 1,000 books you give, you earn {{renown_per_thousand_books}} renown.

Knowledge fades

Every project that holds knowledge erodes every tick, not just the one being researched. The rate starts at {{decay_start_pct}}% a tick. For every tick a project goes without a contribution, its rate rises by {{decay_growth_pct}} percentage points, up to {{decay_max_pct}}% a tick. Any contribution, however small, resets that project to the slow rate. Since books go only to the chosen project, the others keep fading faster.

So a state that keeps feeding its research holds its tiers, while one that forgets loses them gradually, then quickly. Every member's news reports when a tier is reached or lost.
