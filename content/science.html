Science turns books into permanent bonuses. Books come mostly from your scientists, and paper, practice and exploring add more. You spend them on sciences, or give them to your state's Colloquium.

Scientists

  • Scientists work in one of three categories: economy, military or arcane. A new house starts with {{starting_scientists}}, split evenly.
  • Every tick each scientist writes books by rank: {{recruit_books}} as a recruit, rising to {{professor_books}}. Rank comes from how many books their category has written per scientist: {{science_ranks}}.
  • New scientists arrive at {{scientists_per_tick}} a tick (more with universities) and join the category you choose.
  • You can move scientists to another category, but experience stays behind, so the new category's rank drops.

Paper speeds research

You don't need paper to do science, but it helps. Set how much {{paper_material}} your scientists may use each tick. Each unit used adds {{books_per_paper}} books, up to {{paper_cap_pct}}% more than they'd write without it. Only what adds books is used.

Example. A new house writes {{starting_books_per_tick}} books a tick. With a budget of 10 paper it writes {{paper_example_books}} more. Past the cap, extra paper isn't spent.

Learning by doing

  • A successful attack earns up to {{books_per_close_win}} military books, more the closer the fight (defense ÷ offense), like renown.
  • A successful spy earns {{books_per_spy}} arcane books.
  • Each building you start earns {{books_per_building}} economy books.
  • Every 1,000 gold of market fee you pay earns {{books_per_thousand_fee}} economy books. Only an order that fills at once pays the fee, buy or sell, so only that earns books. An order that waits in the book pays none and earns none.

Lost texts

Each time you explore there's a {{lost_text_chance_pct}}% chance your explorers find lost texts: {{books_per_explored_acre}} economy books for every acre explored.

Investing books

Books are spent on sciences in their own category, and take effect at once. A science's bonus grows with the square root of the books in it: four times the books gives twice the bonus. Each science's bonus is held to {{science_cap_pct}}% (a penalty too), however many books it holds. Libraries and other science efficiency then strengthen every science, so the total can go past the cap.

The square root keeps every result a whole number, identical on every machine.
