Your economy updates every tick. All results are rounded down. Modifiers from your race and personality on the same stat add together, then multiply the base amount (for example +5% and +10% give ×1.15).

Income

Gold each tick = peasants × {{gold_per_peasant}} × your income modifier (banks raise it), plus what your banks make. Your state then takes its tax ({{tax_default_pct}}% unless your leader changes it) for the state treasury.

Food

Food grown each tick = what your farms grow (times building efficiency) plus {{barren_food}} for each barren acre, times your food production modifier.

Food eaten each tick = people × {{food_per_person}} × your food consumption modifier. People means peasants plus every troop: at home, away, or in training. Races with no_food eat nothing.

If food runs out, it stays at zero and your house starves each tick until it recovers: you lose {{starvation_pct}}% of your peasants, {{starvation_soldiers_pct}}% of your soldiers, {{starvation_specialists_pct}}% of your specialists and thieves (with their upgrades) and {{starvation_elites_pct}}% of your elites at home, and no new peasants are born. Your news tells you each tick.

Population

Your land holds the living space of every building (see Buildings; homes hold the most), plus {{barren_living}} people per barren acre and {{constructing_living}} per acre under construction, times your population modifier.

Troops take up that room too. Peasants, troops at home, away and in training (thieves included) and medics all count. Scientists, adepts, generals and academics don't. Adepts come out of your peasants, so drawing them frees room. If people outnumber the room, peasants leave at once.

  • Below that, peasants grow by {{peasant_growth_pct}}% a tick (at least 1), up to the limit.
  • Above it, for example after losing land, peasants leave until you are back at the limit.
