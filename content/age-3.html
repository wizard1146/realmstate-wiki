  • -15% your troops killed in battle when you defend (casualties_defending)

  • -16.67% how long armies take to come home at war (return_time, conditional)

  • +35% everything your attacks take (land, plunder, kills) on massacre attacks (attack_gains, conditional)
  • +35% everything your attacks take (land, plunder, kills) on sack attacks (attack_gains, conditional)

  • Upgraded units gain 2 in their other stat.
  • Offensive specialists fight with +1 offense and +1 defense each at war.
  • Elites fight with -1 offense each out of war.
  • Elite+ can be upgraded once more, into elite++.
Peridians new
  • +5% defense of troops at home (defense)
  • -15% gold cost of training troops (training_cost)
  • +15% your troops killed in battle when you defend (casualties_defending)
  • -15% defense of troops at home against troll (defense, conditional)
  • Immune to bribe: it can't be tried on them.
  • kidnap is 15% less likely to work on them.
  • Immune to propaganda: it can't be tried on them.
  • Every peasant at home defends as 20% of a soldier.
  • Every 10 ticks, 0.5% of the soldiers become defensive specialists, free.
Freehomes new
  • -30% time to build (construction_time)
  • -20% building efficiency (can pass 100%) (building_efficiency)
  • -10% offense of troops sent on attacks while well fed (offense, conditional)
  • -10% defense of troops at home while well fed (defense, conditional)
  • Every fighting unit fight with +1 defense each while overpopulated.
  • Well fed means more than 10 food an acre.
  • Training takes 2 ticks less while, within the last 48 ticks, the house explored, took land, or started buildings on 10% of its land.
Unlithians new
  • +20% gold earned from peasants each tick (income)
  • +10% your state's output of its realm's material (material_output)
  • -30% gold paid for mercenaries (mercenary_cost)
  • +30% gold cost of training troops (training_cost)
  • One mercenary for every 3 of your own troops sent.
  • Can't train elites.
  • A won battle turns 0.1% of the surviving offensive specialists into elites.
  • 10% of the mercenaries who survive a battle stay, as offensive specialists.
  • Mercenaries can be hired upgraded, for the upgrade material.
Trolls new
  • -10% troops killed in battle (casualties)
  • +15% how long armies take to come home (return_time)
  • +20% time to train troops, medics and upgrades (training_time)
  • Immune to fires: it can't be tried on them.
  • Immune to undermine: it can't be tried on them.
  • Immune to the torch attack.
  • Immune to the storm hex.
  • Lose 50% fewer buildings in attacks (barren land goes instead).
  • Raze attacks take 10% fewer buildings still.
  • 10% of their battle dead come back after 6 ticks.
Drow new
  • +7% everything your attacks take (land, plunder, kills) (attack_gains)
  • +50% gold cost of exploring (explore_cost)
  • -20% the rate peasants are born at (peasant_growth)
  • +5% offense of troops sent on attacks against ashcliffe (offense, conditional)
  • Raising a general retires 200 elites.
  • At war, an army led by a general with 2+ traits can be sent ready to strike twice: within 3 ticks it strikes again at 50% of its offense, killing 5% of the target's specialists and taking no land. The general is then spent for 72 ticks.
Mycellians new
  • +12% aether gathered each tick (aether_production)
  • -4% books scientists write each tick (book_production)
  • +4% offense of troops sent on attacks against saltborn (offense, conditional)
  • Elites die 8% less in battle.
  • Offensive specialists die 10% more in battle at war.
  • Biohazard: a 25% chance on every attack to afflict the target for 14 ticks: -7% defense of troops at home, -10% the rate peasants are born at, -70% food grown on land each tick.
  • At war, they can take back up to 30% of the land an attack took from them, before the army carrying it gets home, for 10 elites an acre.
Daemons new
  • -33.33% how long armies take to come home (return_time)
  • -5% troops killed in battle (casualties)
  • -15% gold earned from peasants each tick (income)
  • +7% aether rites cost (aether_cost)
  • At war, offense rises 1% every 24 ticks, up to 14%, and falls 1% for every 36 ticks in which they were hit.
  • The house's people and troops eat nothing.
