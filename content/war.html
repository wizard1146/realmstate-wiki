War is fought between states, not single houses. It grows out of fighting: every attack or spy operation across two states makes the victim's state more hostile. Once a state is hostile enough, the other state's leader may declare war. There are two kinds. A general war is between states of different realms. An intra-realm war is between states of the same realm.

The hostility meter

Each state keeps a meter for every other state: how hostile it feels toward that state. Actions by the other state's houses against its houses raise it:

Every {{meter_decay_ticks}} ticks the meter falls by {{meter_decay_pct}}% or {{meter_decay_min_points}} points, whichever is more. It stops at {{hostility_cap}} points.

You can't attack or spy on houses in your own state, or on a state you have a ceasefire with.

The bonus goes to houses attacking a state that feels hostile toward theirs. A state that keeps attacking you makes itself a better target.

Only the two states can see the meters between them (on your home page and at /relations). Wars and ceasefires are public.

Declaring war

Only a state's leader can declare war, and only when all of these hold:

  • Neither state is already at war, and there's no ceasefire between them.
  • Their total land is {{war_range_min_pct}}% to {{war_range_max_pct}}% of yours.
  • At least {{war_unique_attackers}} different houses have attacked across the two states, either way.
  • They are Hostile toward you, and you are at least Unfriendly toward them.

When both states are Hostile toward each other, only the one with the lower meter may declare. When both meters reach {{declare_either_points}}, either side may. When both reach {{auto_war_points}}, war starts on its own at the next tick, whatever the sizes.

At war

  • A war lasts at least {{war_min_ticks}} ticks.
  • Attacks between the two states take {{war_gain_pct}}% more land and earn {{war_renown_pct}}% more renown.
  • Neither state's houses can buy or sell in the Hall of Deeds.
  • A state fights one war at a time.

Ending a war

After the minimum length, either leader can end it:

  • Withdraw. Your state concedes and the other state wins.
  • Offer peace. When both leaders have offered, the war ends in peace. Offering again takes the offer back.

Every war ends with a {{eowcf_ticks}}-tick ceasefire that can't be broken. The meters between the two states start again from nothing.

The winner also takes spoils, depending on the kind of war:

  • General war. For {{tribute_ticks}} ticks, {{tribute_pct}}% of the loser's material output goes to the winner's treasury as tribute.
  • Intra-realm war. The winner takes {{share_transfer_pct}}% of the realm's output share from the loser. A share never falls below {{share_floor_pct}}%, so a state near the floor gives up less.

Example. Two states in realm 4 each hold a {{equal_share_pct}}% share. The first wins an intra-realm war, so its share rises by {{share_transfer_pct}} points and the loser's falls by {{share_transfer_pct}}.

Ceasefires

Leaders can also agree a ceasefire without a war. One leader proposes a length of {{ceasefire_min_ticks}} to {{ceasefire_max_ticks}} ticks. The other accepts by proposing the same length; a different length is a counter-proposal.

A ceasefire blocks attacks and spying between the two states, and clears the meters between them. Either leader may break an agreed ceasefire once {{ceasefire_min_ticks}} ticks have passed. The ceasefire after a war can't be broken.
