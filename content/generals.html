A general leads one army or commands your home defense. Each general has flat traits, such as Aggressive or Strategist, and applies them to the battles it's in.

Raising a general

  • Retire {{general_elites}} elites at home.
  • Pay {{general_cost}} {{general_material}} for each trait.
  • A house keeps at most {{general_max}} generals.

Traits and renown

Your renown decides how many traits a new general can have. The renown needed for the first, second, third trait (and so on) is {{general_trait_renown}}.

Below {{general_pick_renown}} renown, the traits are drawn at random. From {{general_pick_renown}} renown, you choose them.

Example. With 250 renown you have 2 trait slots but can't choose yet: the general gets 2 random traits and costs twice {{general_cost}} {{general_material}}.

In battle

  • One general per army. It goes with the army and comes home with it.
  • One general can command your defense while it's at home. Sending it on an attack ends that command.
  • A general leading a failed attack is killed {{general_death_pct}}% of the time.
