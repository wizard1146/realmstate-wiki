An academic has one or more of these attributes. Each is a flat modifier to your house's science for as long as the academic stays. Attributes of every academic you keep add together.

AttributeEffect
Adept
  • +20% books from learning by doing and lost texts (practice_books)
Prodigious
  • +25% how fast new scientists arrive (scientist_spawn)
Prolific
  • +10% books scientists write each tick (book_production)
Shrewd
  • +25% books each paper adds (paper_books)
Wise
  • +5% strength of every science bonus (science_efficiency)
