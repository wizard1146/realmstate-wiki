Specialised Materials Production: each realm may build the one work for its material, once a season. A state's leader proposes it, and its state votes yes. Each state's leader then votes. It passes once {{realm_work_vote_pct}}% of the realm's states with houses say yes. Then any house in the realm funds it with gold and the realm's material; a gift is capped at what is still needed. Once fully funded, the realm's output of its material rises for the rest of the season. Every house in the realm hears when the work is proposed, passes and is done.
