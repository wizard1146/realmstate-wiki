Realmstate is a strategy game. You run one house. Houses sit together in states, and states are grouped into realms. See The World. The game is played in ages: each age runs for weeks, then everything resets and a new age begins, often with rebalanced rules.

Signing in

Sign in with Google, then add a passkey so later sign-ins are one tap. See Signing In. Each account runs one house per age.

Your house

When you found a house you choose a race and a personality. Together they decide your troops and your strengths. You start with {{starting_land}} acres, {{starting_peasants}} peasants, {{starting_gold}} gold and {{starting_food}} food.

What you do

  • Explore for more land, so you can hold more people.
  • Grow your economy: peasants pay gold every tick and farms feed everyone.
  • Train troops and attack other houses to take their land.

Time

Your economy updates once every tick ({{tick_hours}} hour). Everything else happens the moment you do it, and things you start (explored land, training, armies away) finish at an exact time. See How Time Works.
