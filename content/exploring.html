Exploring buys new land with gold and soldiers. It is the peaceful way to grow, and it gets dearer the bigger you are.

  • Gold: {{explore_gold_per_acre}} an acre, plus {{explore_gold_land_each}} for every acre you already have, times your explore cost modifier.
  • Soldiers: {{explore_soldiers_land_each}} for every acre you already have, per acre explored (rounded up over the order). They settle the land and don't come back. See the draft.
  • For example, {{explore_examples}}.
  • From 1 to 1,000 acres at a time.
  • The land arrives exactly {{explore_ticks}} hours later. Until then it shows as incoming land.
  • Races with no_explore can't explore and must grow by attacking.
