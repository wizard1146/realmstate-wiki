Exploring buys new land with gold and soldiers. It is the peaceful way to grow, and it gets dearer the bigger you are. Each acre also spends one of your explorable acres.

  • Gold: {{explore_gold_per_acre}} an acre, plus {{explore_gold_land_each}} for every acre you already have, plus a part that grows with the square of your land beyond {{explore_square_from}} acres (doubling that extra land roughly quadruples it), times your explore cost modifier. However many cuts stack, exploring never costs less than {{explore_cost_floor_pct}}% of the base gold.
  • Soldiers: {{explore_soldiers_land_each}} for every acre you already have, plus a part growing with the square of your land likewise, per acre explored (rounded up over the order). They settle the land and don't come back. See the draft.
  • For example, {{explore_examples}}.
  • From 1 to 1,000 acres at a time.
  • The land arrives exactly {{explore_ticks}} ticks later. Until then it shows as incoming land.
  • Races with no_explore can't explore and must grow by attacking.

Explorable acres

You can only explore as many acres as you have explorable acres. They grow every tick by a share of your land: {{explorable_max_pct}}% of it while you have {{explorable_from_land}} acres or fewer, falling along a curve to {{explorable_min_pct}}% at {{explorable_to_land}} acres. The curve drops quickly at first and then levels off, so a growing house soon explores at a steady pace rather than in bursts.

The gain is also capped in acres a tick. A bigger house never gets more than a house of {{explorable_from_land}} acres. Past {{explorable_to_land}} acres the gain stops growing: every house that size or bigger gets the same acres a tick as a house of exactly {{explorable_to_land}} acres.

They bank up to {{explorable_bank_ticks}} ticks' worth ({{explorable_bank_days}} days); beyond that they stop growing until you explore. A new house starts with a full bank. For example, {{explorable_examples}}.

You can send explorable acres to a house of your state as aid, to help it grow faster.

While your state is at war, explorable acres grow at {{war_explore_allowance_pct}}% of the usual rate, and exploring costs {{war_explore_cost_pct}}% of the usual gold and soldiers.
