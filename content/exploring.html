Exploring buys new land with gold. It is the peaceful way to grow.

  • Cost: acres × {{explore_gold_per_acre}} gold, times your explore cost modifier.
  • From 1 to 1,000 acres at a time.
  • The land arrives exactly {{explore_ticks}} hours later. Until then it shows as incoming land.
  • Races with no_explore can't explore and must grow by attacking.
