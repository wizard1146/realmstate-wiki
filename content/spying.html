Other houses' gold, food, peasants and troops are hidden (see What You Can See). Spying is how you find them out. You send thieves, the fourth unit slot, at a target house.

Chance of success

It depends on thieves per acre: yours sent, against theirs at home.

  • Equal thieves per acre: {{spy_base_success_pct}}%.
  • More than them raises the chance; fewer lowers it. Formally: {{spy_base_success_pct}}% × 2 × yours ÷ (yours + theirs).
  • It never goes above 95% or below 5%. A target with no thieves at home is spied on at 95%.

If it works

You get a full report of the house as it is right now: land, incoming land, peasants, gold, food, troops at home, troops away and troops in training. The target is not told.

Everyone in your state can read the report too. It stays in your state's reports.

If it fails

{{spy_loss_pct}}% of the thieves you sent are caught (at least one). The target's news says how many were caught, and which house sent them.

Stealth and other thievery operations come in a later milestone. For now, spying has no cost beyond the thieves you risk.
