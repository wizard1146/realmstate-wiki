Terms grouped by topic, as in the sidebar. Link to a term with its name, such as glossary.html#tick.

The World

Address
Where a house sits, written realm:state:house, such as 4:2:1.
House
What one player runs: land, peasants, gold, food and troops. One per account per age.
Realm
A group of states ({{states_per_realm}} each). Realms are the top level of the world map. See The World.
State
A group of houses (up to {{houses_per_state}}) that play together.

Economy

Barren land
Land with no building. It houses a few people and grows a little food.
Building efficiency
How well your buildings are staffed, 50-100%. See Land and Construction.
Clearing price
The single price a market book trades at each tick.
Material
A resource each realm produces every tick. See Materials.
Share
A state's part of its realm's production. See Materials and Trade.
Treasury
A state's own gold and materials, run by its leader.

Science

Academic
A named scholar with flat attributes that strengthen your science; one per {{academic_universities}} universities. See Academics.
Books
Science points, written by scientists and earned by learning by doing. See Science.
Colloquium
A state's shared research: the leader picks a project, members give books. See Colloquium.
Scientist
Writes books every tick in the economy, military or arcane category, more as it gains rank.

War

Adept
A non-fighting unit, drawn from peasants at shrines, that works rites.
Aether
What rites spend; shrines gather it every tick. See Rites.
Ceasefire
A spell with no attacks or spying between two states. One follows every war. See War.
General
A commander raised from elites, with flat traits; one per army.
Hex
A rite that harms another house. See Rites and Hexes.
Hostility meter
How hostile one state feels toward another, raised by that state's attacks and spying. Sets the relation: Normal, Unfriendly or Hostile. See War.
Medic
A non-fighting unit that saves battle deaths. See Military.
Renown
Public standing won in battle. Unlocks better generals.
Tribute
Part of a losing state's material output paid to the winner of a general war. See War.
War points
A state's score from wars: {{war_win_points}} for a win, {{war_peace_points}} for peace. See War.

Information

Courting
An operation that makes another house's general or academic waver; its owner can reassure it. See Intrigue.
False Flag
Sending an operation under another state's colours. See Intrigue.
Nerve
What your thieves' operations spend; it recovers every tick. See Intrigue.
News
Your house's private feed of what happened to it. See What You Can See.
Operation
One thing your thieves do: intel, theft or sabotage. See Thieves' Operations.
Spy report
What a successful intel operation learned about another house. Shared with your state.
Vigil
A rite a whole state funds together; every member gains while it lasts. See Rites and Hexes.
Ward
Resistance to hexes and divinations, from watchstones. See Rites.
Vigilance
How alert a house is after recent operations against it; it lowers the next one's chance. See Intrigue.

Time and Ages

Age
One round of the game, from start to reset. Each age has its own frozen rules. See All Ages.
Tick
The hourly economy update: income, food and population. See How Time Works.

How the rules are recorded

Definition
A race or personality's numbers and effects for an age, such as orc@v2. A rebalance is a new definition of the same identity.
Effect
One building block of a race or personality: a modifier, a flag, an unlock or its unit slots. See Effects.
Identity
A race or personality's permanent name, such as orc. It never changes, so history can follow it across ages.
Lineage
A reworked race gets a new identity that points back to the one it replaced (for example Revenant from Undead).
Rules fingerprint
A short code computed from a definition's mechanics. Same fingerprint, same rules, even across ages.
Slot
A numbered place an age puts a definition into (15 for races, 8 for personalities). Slots can be left unused. Only the current age uses them; history records identities.
