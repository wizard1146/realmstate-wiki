Lore

To the Peridian, the State is everything: the State is Father, Mother, Family and Child. All Peridians are near incorruptible and require no oaths of fealty; they bleed purple, and will bleed to death for their State and Realm without question. Skilled with long-range weapons, they prefer to inflict mass casualties on their foes before going in for the kill.
