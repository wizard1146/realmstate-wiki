Each state can have a leader, chosen and removed by a simple majority of its houses. The leader runs the state's treasury.

Voting

  • Your house has one standing vote: for a house in your state (yours included), against the current leader, or none.
  • Votes are counted every tick. A house backed by more than half the state's houses becomes leader.
  • A leader opposed by more than half the state's houses loses the post. Opposed means voting against them, or for someone else.
  • Votes stay until you change them, so a leader stays in office only while the state backs them.

Everyone in the state hears when a leader is chosen or removed. Other states can see who leads each state on the realm page; only members see the vote counts.

What the leader can do

  • Set the state's tax.
  • Buy and sell on the market with the treasury's gold and materials.
  • Grant treasury gold or materials to houses in the state.

Everything else about a house stays with its owner.

State chat

Every state has its own chat, for its members only. Messages arrive live on your house page. It keeps the age's whole conversation.

Tax

Each tick, the state takes a share of every member's income and pays it into the treasury. The leader sets the rate anywhere from {{tax_min_pct}}% to {{tax_max_pct}}%; a new state starts at {{tax_default_pct}}%. A change applies from the next tick, and every member's news reports it.
